III. Use of Data with Copyright Restrictions Copyright status can be found directly beneath each graph and map on FRED, and the notes below the graph and map provide more information. If you are unsure if a data series is under copyright, please contact us. There are three (3) types of data series copyright status you will encounter in FRED: Copyrighted: Pre-approval required These series are under copyright by a third party. Without first obtaining the express written permission of the copyright holder, the information may only be, these series may only be used for non-commercial educational or personal use. For any other use, you must obtain permission from the copyright holder and include proper attribution of ownership. Neither the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis nor FRED can provide permission(s) on behalf of copyright holders and will not request such permission(s) on your behalf. Link to these series . Copyrighted: Citation required These series are under copyright; but, provided you have not engaged in any prohibited uses, you may use these data series with proper attribution of the source and acknowledgment that you obtained the data from FRED (example, “ Source: BLS via FRED ”) when displaying or publishing it. We recommend using the citation provided in the series notes. Graphs and maps downloaded from FRED are self-citing and do not require a separate citation. Link to these series . Public Domain: Citation requested These series may be under copyright or in the public domain and may be used without permission, provided you do not engage in any prohibited use. When using, please cite the data source and acknowledge that you obtained the data from FRED (example, “ Source: BLS via FRED ”) when displaying or publishing it. We recommend using the citation provided in the series notes. Graphs and maps downloaded from FRED are self-citing and do not require a separate citation. Quite a few series are in the public domain.
