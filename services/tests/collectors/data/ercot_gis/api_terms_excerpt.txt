or using ERCOT’s API, you are agreeing to the below Terms of Use. The information necessary to create an account includes, but is not limited to, providing email address, and name. This information is required to provide ERCOT with analytics for budgeting and future site planning.  ERCOT may require additional information as it deems necessary regarding the user or account. Accounts registered by "bots" or other automated methods are not permitted. You are responsible for protecting your log-in information and shall not share that information with other parties. B. Permitted Access You will only access (or attempt to access) the ERCOT API by the means described in these Terms of Use. Violations to ERCOT's Terms of Use Policy This policy applies to both: Automated screen scraping programs that access ERCOT URL(s);  Programs that use the Data Access Portal to query and download report data via the Data Access Portal or External Web Service (EWS); and Use of ERCOT's API in any other way. The objectives of this policy are to: Prevent overloading of ERCOT computing resources that may occur due to excessive queries. Ensure optimal transactional request/response performance to all Market Participants. Ensure a free exchange of information to benefit Market Participants. Note: Query submission at a faster rate will not provide additional benefits to the Market Participant. For technical issues, or questions or clarifications related to this policy, please submit a ticket via the ERCOT Public Portal . (New users will be prompted to register in order to log in and submit a help ticket.) Additional Terms of Use Specific to Data Access Portal ERCOT created its Data Access Portal site to assist users with enabling the automated download of public reports.  ERCOT publishes a list called the ERCOT Market Information List (EMIL) , which describes the public reports that are regularly published by ERCOT on its website, as well as the dates that the reports are generated, posted, and archived, which should be consulted when determining your timing of automated downloads.  Data Access Portal General Limitations ERCOT reserves the right to set and enforce limits on your use of the Data Access Portal in ERCOT’s sole discretion For example, ERCOT imposes limitations on the number of times a report may be downloaded per year, as described below. You agree to, and will not attempt to circumvent, such limitations.   If you would like to use the Data Access Portal beyond these limits, you must obtain ERCOT's express written consent (ERCOT may refuse such request or condition approval  of the request on your agreement to additional terms and/or charges for that use). To submit a request to exceed these limits, contact ERCOT Client Services at clientservices@ercot.com. Limitations on Downloads You may not download the same report from the Data Access Portal more than three times within a 12-month period. Reports should be downloaded and stored on the users’ own hardware to avoid downloading from the site multiple times during a yearly time period.
