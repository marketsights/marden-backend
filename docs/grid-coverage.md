# Grid coverage

Which power regions Marden covers, where each region's interconnection queue (and large-load requests, where
the operator publishes them) comes from, which collector reads it, how often, and its status. Regions live in
`grid_region` (db/migrations/20261021150000_grid_region.sql): interconnections, ISO/RTOs, balancing
authorities (BAs), retail utilities, European TSOs and NERC reliability regions. Every campus, building, site
and power plant with a location gets `interconnection`, `iso`, `balancing_authority`, `utility` and
`utility_territories` facts from the spatial join (`grid_join`): by its own point, else a stand-in point (its
campus, its one approved linked campus, its one served campus), else its county polygon (`census_counties`).

Status: **built** (collector runs and writes data) · **needs key** (free registration key required; the
collector stays inert until `<SOURCE>_API_KEY` is set) · **blocked** (terms forbid automated collection; the
source row is marked blocked) · **unreachable** (a bot challenge or client-side request signing refuses every
automated request; not bypassed, each run records it) · **V2** (region registered, no collector yet).

official queue page; the collector, cadence and status cells are left for the collector that reads it.
Collectors that are not queues (capacity auctions, load forecasts, large-load reports) go in section 3.

### 2.3 US balancing authorities outside the ISO/RTOs

Every active US BA in EIA-930 that is not an ISO/RTO BA (the eight ISO/RTO BAs are in 2.2). Interconnection
requests in these areas go to the transmission provider under its OATT (FERC Order 2023; TVA, SRP and Santee
Cooper through reciprocity tariffs) and are posted on its OASIS. Checked 2026-10-21 for the providers serving
data-center growth areas:

- **Own website:** BPA posts its whole queue, including line and load (large-load) requests, as a workbook
  (`bpa_queue`, built). El Paso Electric posts its active generator queue as a PDF (`epe_queue`, collected under the business
  owner's decision despite EPE's noncommercial terms; flagged unlicensed-commercial in `marden.core.licensing`). PNM's site has only the distribution-level Rule 568 queue.
- **OATI webOASIS only:** Southern (SOCO), Duke (DUK), TVA, Santee Cooper (SC), Dominion Energy South Carolina
  (SCEG), PacifiCorp (PPW), APS (AZPS), SRP, TEP (TEPC), NV Energy (NEVP), PNM, EPE, PSCo (PSCO), Idaho Power
  (IPCO), PGE and PSE (PSEI). `oati_oasis` holds the shared pieces (OATI's root CA, which the system bundle lacks;
  the public paths) and checks the 16 nodes monthly: all answer, but the folder tree that lists their
  documents (and so the queue files) is served only to webSmartOASIS calls signed by the browser client
  (rcpt/rcpi checksum headers), which Marden does not emulate, so it records the gap and writes nothing.
- **National cross-check:** `lbnl_queued_up` loads LBNL / GridTracker's "Queued Up" dataset (2026 edition,
  generation requests through 2025-12-31, CC BY 4.0 with attribution) as tag R, source `LBNL Queued Up`, on
  separate subjects `lbnl:<entity>:<queue id>` so it never displaces an operator's own entry; `operator_entry`
  points to that entry when Marden has it. Live 2026-10-21: 38,201 entries (25,770 in the ISO/RTOs, 12,431
  outside), 16,917 cross-linked to operator entries (PJM 7,666, SPP 2,837, CAISO 2,278, NYISO 1,918, ISO-NE
  1,220, BPA 998; ERCOT on LBNL's next run, its entries exist since 2026-10-21; MISO once its entries exist), 2 matched: ERCOT 14INR0033 and 23INR0637 (Goodnight
  Wind I and II, FGE Goodnight I/II LLC, Armstrong County TX) to campus `goodnight` on the phrase "goodnight",
  which is also the place name, so treat it as a weak link to review. LBNL has no load requests.
- **Entergy** has no queue outside MISO: all five Entergy operating companies (Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi,
  New Orleans, Texas) are MISO transmission owners. **Dominion's** only non-PJM system is Dominion Energy
  South Carolina (SCEG).

## 4. Known gaps

- **Boundary vintages.** The ISO/RTO footprints are from 2022 and the control areas from 2025-04-08, so SPP RTO
  West (EIA-930 BA SWPW, 2026-04-01), Black Hills (BHBA, 2026-04-01) and Sikeston (SIKE, 2025-06-01) have no
  boundary yet, and WACM/WAUW still have theirs. The join turns a point's `balancing_authority`, and its
  `iso` when the retirement is newer than the ISO layer, into a candidate with a review item whenever the BA
  is retired per EIA-930. New HIFLD Next versions are picked up by the monthly run.
- **NERC Regions** were sourced in 2010 (validated 2022-06-03) and still show FRCC and SPP RE; they are used only
  to derive interconnection boundaries. Seams are checked against EIA-930: if a BA containing the point belongs
  to another interconnection, the `interconnection` fact waits for review (Lubbock-area sites, the Texas
  panhandle), and so does every BA and ISO/RTO (by its EIA-930 BAs) that the NERC union contradicts. Example:
  HIFLD draws all of Andrews County, TX inside SWPP/SPP while the NERC union and the site's filings (Oncor) say
  ERCOT, so Stingray's interconnection, ISO and BA all wait for a person.
- **Overlaps.** ISO and BA polygons overlap on seams (ERCOT/SPP in the Texas panhandle, MISO/SPP in North
  Dakota): each candidate is written and one review item opened per subject and field. Retail territories are
  mostly drawn at county resolution, so co-ops, municipals and IOUs overlap: `utility` is published only when one
  territory contains the point; otherwise `utility_territories` lists them (on 2026-10-21, 1 of 24 placed V1
  sites had a single territory: Jupiter, El Paso Electric). The serving utility is in each site's filing-based
  `power_utility` fact and each campus's `utility` fact (seed).
- **Subjects without a point.** Sites with a county but no point are placed by the county polygon (Black Pearl,
  Winkler County; Stingray, Andrews County; CoreWeave DDTL-3, Denton County). A county split between regions, or
  partly outside every ISO/RTO, waits for review. CoreWeave DDTL-4 (four undisclosed sites) has no location and no
  grid facts. None of the 74 power plants has a point or county yet: 10 that serve one campus in their own state
  take that campus's point (interconnection, ISO, BA; no utility); the other 64, including n-xai-southaven
  (Southaven, MS, serving Colossus 2 in Memphis, TN), wait for a location (EIA-860M plant coordinates or permit
  addresses).
- **Outside the US** no boundaries are loaded; Canadian and European rows are registrations only.
- **Queues without names** (SPP; CAISO has project names but no customer) cannot meet queue-match@0.1, so
  entries in our counties stay unmatched even when they sit next to a site: e.g. SPP ERAS-2025-002 (217 MW
  combustion turbine, "Abernathy", TX, at Xcel's Tuco 345 kV, IA pending) near Abernathy, and SPP entries in
  Muskogee County, OK (Core Scientific Muskogee). Linking those needs POI or coordinate evidence.
- **Queue matching** (`iso_queue_match`, queue-match@0.1). Queues publish no coordinates, so an entry links to
  one of our sites, campuses or plants only on state + county + a distinctive name of ours (site, alias,
  SPV, developer, tenant) in the entry's project name or customer; the evidence is in the link fact. Entries
  filed under other names stay unmatched, e.g. NYISO 0849 "Somerset Load" (Somerset Operating Company, 250 MW,
  in service at Kintigh 345 kV, the Lake Mariner substation). Campus names that carry the developer ("TeraWulf
  Lake Mariner") must match whole: NYISO 1670 "Lake Mariner Data II" links to site `lake-mariner` but not to
  campus `terawulf-lake-mariner` (1732 links both, on "terawulf"). Links are recomputed on every run, so campuses
  loaded after a queue run are linked by the next one. MISO, home to Hyperion, River Bend, Lighthouse,
  The Barn and Polaris Forge, is not reachable (2.2).
- **Non-RTO queues** (2.3): 16 OATI-hosted providers' queue files cannot be listed (signed webSmartOASIS calls);
  EPE's own PDF is blocked by its terms; TVA's and SRP's websites refuse automated clients. Until a provider
  gives a stable public file URL (add it to `oati_oasis.QUEUE_DOCUMENTS` with a parser) these areas are covered
  only by LBNL Queued Up, a yearly snapshot (R) without large loads; status or COD changes after 2025-12-31 and
  every large-load request there are missing. The LBNL lines `lbnl:aps:not assigned` and ids with `/<date>`
  suffixes are LBNL's own repeated or missing queue numbers kept apart.
