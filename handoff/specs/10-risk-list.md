# 10 · Risk list (`/risk-list?week=`)

**Job:** every Monday, the instruments most likely to reprice in the next 90 days, with the record that put each one there, and a short written note.
**Reference:** screens/10-risk-list.html · **Endpoint:** `GET /api/risk-list?week=YYYY-MM-DD`

## Layout

1. Header: "Risk list", subtitle "Published every Monday." Week selector (‹ Oct 13–19, 2026 ›), Excel, Email the note.
2. Table (8–15 rows):
   - Rank (black square).
   - Instrument and why it is here: name (link) + size on line 1; line 2 = **date** + the triggering record + tags.
   - Price · 1w: Marden price + change.
   - Fund mark · gap: average filed mark · gap (red if ≥ 5 pts), or "no fund holds it", "traded", "private", "n/d".
   - Odds of a 3+ point move in 90 days: OddsBar (M). Header reads "· model v0" until the backtest calibrates the model.
   - vs last week: ▲ n / ▼ n / — / New.
3. This week's note: 4 short paragraphs, each starting with a bold name, each sentence linked to its record. "Drafted by Marden's agent, checked before sending."
4. Earlier calls: dated past calls with lead time; link to Track record.

## Rules

- Rank by odds, ties broken by debt size. Odds come from the repricing model (M).
- The list and note are frozen when published; past weeks are read-only snapshots.
- The note is generated by the AI agent from the list and must pass a human check before email goes out.

## Acceptance

- Selecting an older week shows that week's list exactly as published.
- "Email the note" sends to the user only (the weekly email to all users is scheduled separately).
